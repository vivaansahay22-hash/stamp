Owner's note

The product should plan with people, not talk at them.

The core AI of the product will act as the user's personal scheduling engine rather than simply functioning as a chatbot. It will interpret calendar entries, identify whether activities are flexible or rigid, understand deadlines and priorities, and automatically create an efficient schedule. When a user's calendar changes, the AI can reorganize flexible activities while protecting fixed commitments. It will also use the resulting schedule to determine appropriate reminders and alarms. The goal is to reduce the amount of manual planning required from students and adults while keeping the user in control of their schedule.
